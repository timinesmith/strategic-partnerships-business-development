# Sponsorship Acquisition Case Study — Athlete Development

## Need

Amateur boxer Richard "The Menace" Johnson needed sponsorship support to sustain his amateur career. At that level, training, travel, and competition costs are commonly carried by the athlete and their support network.

## Timothy's role

Independent sponsorship and partnership development, 2021.

## Approach

1. Identified attorney Michael van der Veen as a well-aligned prospective sponsor.
2. Approached the sponsor with a focused case for the athlete's development, visibility, and community value.
3. Discussed and negotiated terms.
4. Closed the sponsorship agreement.

## Result

**$10,000 sponsorship secured** in 2021 to support Johnson's amateur career.

## Why the case matters

The value is not only the dollar amount. It demonstrates a complete, transferable sponsorship cycle — prospecting, fit assessment, pitch, negotiation, and close — that can be applied to sports, entertainment, nonprofit, venue, and community-program partnerships.
