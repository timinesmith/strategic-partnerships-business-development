# Strategic Partnerships & Business Development

## Executive summary

A partnership-development portfolio covering prospect research, sponsorship design, outreach sequencing, pipeline management, and negotiation preparation. It includes Timothy Nesmith's verified 2021 sponsorship acquisition case.

## Business problem

Organizations often pursue sponsors and partners opportunistically: no clear audience fit, no defined asset package, no follow-up system, and no measurable next step. A disciplined pipeline turns relationship development into a managed business process.

## Scope

- Ideal sponsor/partner profile
- Prospect research and fit scoring
- Sponsorship package structure
- Outreach and follow-up cadence
- Pipeline stages and decision tracking
- Agreement-readiness checklist

## Role and evidence basis

**Real case basis:** In 2021, Timothy independently identified and approached attorney Michael van der Veen, presented the case for supporting amateur boxer Richard "The Menace" Johnson, negotiated terms, and secured a $10,000 sponsorship.

**Demonstration status: Illustrative demonstration.** The framework and tracker templates are newly created portfolio tools. Sample pipeline rows are illustrative and do not represent current prospects.

## Methods and tools

Prospect research, fit scoring, value-proposition design, sponsorship packaging, stakeholder mapping, pipeline management, negotiation preparation, and business-development reporting.
