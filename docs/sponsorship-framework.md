# Sponsorship Development Framework

**Status:** Illustrative framework informed by a verified sponsorship acquisition case.

## 1. Define the property

Clarify the athlete, event, program, or organization being supported; the audience; the geography; the timeframe; and the specific use of funds.

## 2. Build the asset inventory

- Naming or logo visibility
- Event or content presence
- Hospitality or participation access
- Community-impact story
- Media and social content opportunities
- Category exclusivity, if actually available

Do not offer inventory the property does not control.

## 3. Score prospect fit

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Audience | Does the sponsor value this audience? |
| Geography | Is the activity in the sponsor's market? |
| Values | Is the story credible for the sponsor's brand? |
| Timing | Is there a decision window? |
| Capacity | Can the sponsor fund at the proposed level? |
| Activation | Can the sponsor use the assets, not merely receive them? |

## 4. Make the approach

Lead with fit and outcome, not desperation. State the request, the use of funds, the sponsor benefit, and the decision timeline.

## 5. Negotiate and document

Confirm amount, payment timing, deliverables, approval rights, term, renewal discussion, and reporting. Put the agreement in writing.

## 6. Fulfill and report

Deliver every promised asset, document proof of performance, and report results before asking for renewal.
